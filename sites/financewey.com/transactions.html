Compare structures by what they cost in value.

The amount of capital raised is only one line in the equation. Control, dilution, cash priority, collateral, future upside and retained liabilities can matter just as much.

STRUCTURING OPTIONS

One economic basis. Different instruments.

FinanceWey helps compare structures on a common model so the implicit concessions and long-term value transfer are visible before a decision is made.

01 / INDUSTRIAL

JV, earn-in & farm-out

Capital or work commitments in exchange for participation, control rights and future value. Focus: entry valuation, funding obligations, dilution and execution capacity.

02 / PROJECT DEBT

Project financing

Debt sized to project cash flows and completion risk. Focus: debt service, security, reserve accounts, covenants and downside resilience.

03 / RESERVES

Reserve-backed structures

Where relevant, borrowing capacity linked to eligible reserves and net revenues. Focus: borrowing-base re-determination, liquidity and coverage risk.

04 / PRODUCTION

Pre-financing & off-take

Upfront liquidity against future sales or deliveries. Focus: pricing, committed volumes, commercial flexibility, delivery risk and counterparty exposure.

05 / ROYALTY

Royalty & streaming

Upfront capital in exchange for a durable share of revenue or production economics. Focus: base, duration, buyback, seniority and effect on residual equity value.

06 / M&A

Acquisition & divestment support

Decision support for full or partial asset transactions. Focus: value range, transaction perimeter, liabilities, data-room readiness and offer comparison.

NEGOTIATION LOGIC

Define the limits before the negotiation starts.

For each structure we can define acceptable value ranges, control thresholds, funding commitments, conditions precedent, security boundaries and residual risks. That turns a transaction process into a managed set of trade-offs.

AValue retained

What economic upside remains with the asset owner after funding costs and concessions?

BRisk transferred

Which technical, completion, market or financing risks genuinely move to the counterparty?

CControl preserved

Which governance, budget, audit, veto and remedy rights remain available?

DFuture flexibility

What does the structure do to refinancing, additional partners, future sales and strategic optionality?
