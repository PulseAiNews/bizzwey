WHAT WE DO

A decision framework for complex assets.

FinanceWey connects the technical, economic, contractual and financial dimensions of an asset so that owners and decision-makers can negotiate from a coherent position.

DILIGENCEVALUATIONSTRUCTURINGCAPITALRISKNEGOTIATION
01 / DIAGNOSTIC

Asset & rights diagnostic

Start with what is actually owned, what is already committed and which information gaps can materially change value.

  • Rights, licences, interests, obligations and transfer constraints
  • Existing debt, guarantees, security, off-take or revenue commitments
  • Data-room review and prioritisation of high-impact diligence items
02 / TECHNICAL

Transaction-oriented technical diligence

Coordinate the review of the assumptions that drive economic value rather than treating technical work as a separate exercise.

  • Mining resources, grades, recovery, development sequence and processing assumptions
  • Oil & gas reserves, production profiles, decline, integrity and abandonment obligations
  • Infrastructure capacity, completion risk, availability, operating assumptions and lifecycle capex
03 / VALUE

Asset valuation & economic scenarios

Build a transparent range of value and expose the variables that change it.

  • Cash-flow modelling, fiscal regimes, working capital and end-of-life obligations
  • Central, downside and sensitivity cases
  • Project value, equity value, debt and priority claims reconciliation
04 / STRUCTURE

Contractual & financial structuring

Compare different ways to fund or monetise the asset on a common economic basis.

  • Industrial partnerships, JV, earn-in and farm-out structures
  • Project debt, reserve-backed financing support and production pre-financing
  • Royalty or streaming structures where appropriate to the asset and jurisdiction
05 / RISK

Risk allocation & protections

Identify which risks can be transferred, which must be retained and which protections are needed for the transaction to remain acceptable.

  • Governance, control, budgets, overruns and remediation rights
  • Security packages, cash controls, recourse and inter-creditor considerations
  • Environmental, community, integrity and end-of-life liabilities
06 / EXECUTION

Transaction preparation & negotiation support

Turn the analysis into a practical decision and negotiation file.

  • Opportunity materials and data-room structure
  • Offer comparison, negotiation parameters and concession tracking
  • Coordination with technical, legal, tax, environmental and regulated specialists
