CAPITAL & STRATEGIC PARTNERS

Make the opportunity legible to the right counterparties.

FinanceWey helps owners and sponsors translate a technically complex asset into a clear strategic proposition before approaching industrial partners, lenders, investors or other qualified counterparties.

MARKET ACCESS

Not a contact list. A qualified process.

The work begins with the asset and the transaction logic. Counterparty outreach only becomes useful once the value proposition, structure, information pack and decision parameters are coherent.

01 / POSITION

Opportunity definition

Clarify the asset, transaction perimeter, capital need, strategic rationale, key economics, decision timeline and information that can be shared.

02 / MAP

Counterparty mapping

Identify industrial operators, strategic investors, specialist funds, lenders or commercial partners whose mandate and capabilities fit the opportunity.

03 / QUALIFY

Fit & capability screening

Assess strategic fit, financial capacity, technical credibility, geographic appetite, transaction history and likely decision path.

04 / PREPARE

Materials & data readiness

Prepare a coherent teaser, information memorandum logic, data-room structure, valuation reference points and controlled disclosure process.

05 / ENGAGE

Introductions & process support

Coordinate qualified introductions, questions, information requests and process discipline while preserving a consistent transaction narrative.

06 / NEGOTIATE

Offer comparison & negotiation

Compare proposals on a common economic basis, track concessions and support the owner’s advisers through the commercial negotiation.
